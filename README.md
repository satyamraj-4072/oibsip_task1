# 📢 Hiring Poster Design – Next Gen Solutions

A modern and eye-catching **recruitment poster design** created for **Next Gen Solutions**. This project showcases a clean and professional hiring advertisement designed to attract potential candidates through bold typography, vibrant colors, and an organized layout.

---

## 📖 Overview

This hiring poster is designed with a contemporary corporate style that effectively communicates recruitment information. The design highlights open positions, company branding, and contact details while maintaining a visually appealing and easy-to-read layout.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎨 Modern and professional poster design
- 📢 Bold "We're Hiring" headline
- 🏢 Corporate branding with company logo
- 🖋️ Clean typography and visual hierarchy
- 📋 Organized job listings
- 🖼️ High-quality imagery
- 🧡 Attractive orange and dark teal color palette
- 📱 Suitable for both social media and print
